## Dataset and training protocol

- **UCI CCPP dataset:** 9,568 hourly observations, five columns; AT (°C), V (cm Hg), AP (mbar), RH (%) → PE (MW).
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0, Tüfekci and Kaya, DOI https://doi.org/10.24432/C5002N. Data were converted from the original XLSX to CSV.
- **Split:** first 7,654 training rows, final 1,914 test rows; 41 exact duplicates retained for course comparability. Sixteen distinct test rows match training rows, which may overstate generalization.
- **Model:** StandardScaler + KNeighborsRegressor, five-fold training-only CV, k search [1,3,5,10,20], random_state=0; k=3 selected.
- **Result:** held-out test k-NN MAE **2.743109 MW** versus training-mean baseline **14.723152 MW**. Figures in `figures/` and `results.json` are generated directly by `lab/knn_project.py`.

## Numerical method #4

Stated assumptions: ideal single-diode cell with Iph=5 A, I0=10^-10 A, n=1.30, T=298.15 K, Rs=0, Rsh=∞. Newton–Raphson gives Vmp **0.7188038973 V**, Imp **4.7779834876 A** and Pmp **3.4344331522 W**. The derivative and result were checked independently by finite differences, golden-section power maximization and a dense power-grid search. Plot and iteration table are in [numerical.md](numerical.md), and generated by `code/numerical.py`.
