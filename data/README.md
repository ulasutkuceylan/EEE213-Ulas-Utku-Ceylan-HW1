# CCPP dataset

`ccpp.csv`: 9,568 full-load hourly averages from a combined cycle power plant (2006–2011).

Columns: AT (°C), V (cm Hg), AP (mbar), RH (%), PE (MW).

**Creators:** P. Tüfekci and H. Kaya. **Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository, DOI https://doi.org/10.24432/C5002N. **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. **Change:** original XLSX converted to CSV for this course; data columns and row ordering retained.

Known limitations: 41 exact duplicate rows and a relative-humidity maximum of 100.16%. No missing values. Scope of results is full-load operation; transfer to other plants or partial-load conditions requires independent validation.
